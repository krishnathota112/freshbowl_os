### ⚠ What is proved and what still needs eyes

No DOM environment is installed, so structural claims are asserted against the route's source and its
pure data, as in C2. B.1's ordering, B.3's count, B.6's URL and B.8's absence of arithmetic are all
structural and now enforced. **The appearance halves are not**: that the band reads as urgent, that the
diagonal reads as a diagonal, and that nothing scrolls sideways at 375 px in a real browser. Those need
the five-minute pass, in both themes, at 375/768/1280 px.

### Two of these tests were wrong first, in an instructive way

Three assertions failed on the **file's own prose**. `/chart/i` matched a comment quoting §9.2's *"there
is no fifth number and no chart"*; `/percent/i` matched the on-screen sentence explaining why there is
no percentage; and `/rest does not compress/` failed because JSX had wrapped the sentence across two
lines.

Searching prose for banned words tests the prose. The scans now run on source with comments stripped
and check for the things that would have to be **present to render** a chart or a fraction, and the
prose check runs on flattened whitespace. Explaining a ban is not breaking it.
