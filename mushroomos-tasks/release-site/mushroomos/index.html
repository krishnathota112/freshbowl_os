Download APK

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open it from Downloads. If Android asks, allow your browser to install this app.
  3. If MushroomOS is already installed, choose Update. Do not uninstall it — you would lose nothing on the server, but you would have to sign in again.

Older copies (1.0, 1.1) need this update manually once. After that, open Account → Update in the app to check for new releases. Android always asks you to confirm installation.
