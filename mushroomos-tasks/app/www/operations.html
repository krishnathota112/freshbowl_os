At a glance

On the field now

All running tasks are shown; parallel work is not collapsed into a single stage.

Process map

Functional stages, not a physical floor plan. No bunker or tunnel occupancy is inferred.

Observed actual time by task

Historical average of completed tasks with both actual start and finish times, grouped by task name, stream and Turner pass. The sample count (n) shows how many observations support each average. These are not Process Time SOP standards.

Manpower by task

Average recorded crew and observed labour-hours. Coverage exposes missing crew counts or actual times; people are not deduplicated across tasks.

Machines and fuel

Kilometres come from paired task odometer readings. Litres are measured at refuelling, not allocated to tasks.

Task ledger

Current active batches plus tasks finished in the selected period. Planned and actual timestamps stay separate.
